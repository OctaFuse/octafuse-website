**Audit Logs** focuses on user, key and quota events. Combine it with [request logs](/en/docs/guides/request-logs/); it is not a complete audit trail of every provider or route configuration edit.

## Investigate an event

1. Filter by time, event type, source, reason and actor. Add email, Actor ID or correlation ID as needed.
2. Distinguish periodic budget consumption, wallet consumption and credit, resets and user/key lifecycle events.
3. Expand a record for snapshot differences and reasons.
4. Follow a related request ID to check the charge. Trace top-ups through the user's credit history and business reference.

The list summarizes event, identity and quota change; details show before/after values and the raw snapshot. Per-request charge events are excluded by default; use the charge shortcut ranges when you need them, and jump from a record to its related request.

![Combined audit filters](/screenshots/v2-10-0-audit-log-filters.webp)

## Reconcile both pools

A request may consume both pools, so inspect their changes separately. Wallet credit should carry a unique business reference. A periodic reset does not clear the wallet.

Read [billing](/en/docs/guides/billing/) and the [audit reference](https://github.com/OctaFuse/octafuse-gateway/blob/v2.19.0/docs/developers/reference/user-audit-logs.md) for definitions.
